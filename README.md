# SCEPS: Slot-complete evidence path search for hazardous waste knowledge graph question answering
Hazardous waste question answering is essential for industrial safety and emergency response. While recent LLM-based knowledge graph question answering (KGQA) methods have achieved promising performance across various domains, hazardous waste question answering remains largely unexplored. Unlike conventional knowledge graphs,  hazardous waste knowledge graphs used in this setting are typically organized around sparse chemical-centered structures, where answering a question often requires multiple complementary pieces of evidence. As a result, semantically relevant paths may not provide sufficiently complete evidence for reliable answer generation. To address this challenge, we construct a  hazardous waste knowledge graph from Safety Data Sheets and regulatory resources, and further propose SCEPS (Slot-Complete Evidence Path Search), a KGQA framework that explicitly models evidence completeness during graph reasoning. SCEPS decomposes each query into five evidence slots and verifies whether the retrieved evidence collectively satisfies its information requirements. In addition, SCEPS abstains when sufficient evidence is unavailable. Experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed method. Combined with Qwen3-Max, SCEPS achieves 89.75\% single-hop accuracy and 44.00\% multi-hop accuracy while maintaining a hallucination rate of only 5.33\%, substantially improving evidence completeness and reliability in hazardous waste KGQA.
